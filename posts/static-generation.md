---
title: 'Why Static Generation Makes Websites Fast'
date: '2026-09-16'
---

Static generation creates a page before a visitor asks for it. Next.js can then serve that finished page quickly instead of rebuilding it for every request.

This works well for blogs, documentation, and other content that does not change every second. The build step does the heavy lifting once, so the browser gets a simpler and faster experience.

I think of it as meal prepping for a website: a little planning up front makes serving each visitor much easier.
